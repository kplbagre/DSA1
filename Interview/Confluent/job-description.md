## 🎯 The Role — What You'll Do

**Team:** Tableflow (under Confluent Cloud / IBM Software)

**Mission:** Build the critical components that turn Confluent Cloud data streams into open table formats. Bridge the gap between operational streaming (Kafka) and analytical data lakes (Apache Iceberg, Delta Lake) by building control plane and data plane components that materialize topics into tables in real-time.

### Two Core Pillars

| Pillar | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Control Plane** | Robust orchestration layer managing the entire lifecycle of tables across AWS, Azure, and GCP |
| **Open Table Formats & Ecosystem** | Translation logic to publish Kafka data as Tables (Apache Iceberg, Delta Lake) + integration with lakehouse ecosystem (AWS Glue, Unity Catalog) |

### Day-to-Day Responsibilities

1. Design and implement **data plane components** that publish data into open-table formats and integrate with the ecosystem
2. Develop **control plane services and APIs** that orchestrate table lifecycles and resource management
3. Contribute to relevant **open-source projects** such as Apache Iceberg
4. Own **code quality, test coverage, documentation, and maintainability** for your components
5. Drive **technical designs**, leading discussions to curate and moderate decisions with stakeholders
6. Collaborate **cross-functionally** with Product, Design, and other engineering teams to deliver complex features
7. Lead **operational excellence** — resolving high-severity incidents, building observability tools, designing automated workflows for **99.99% availability**
8. Be an active team player — leading meetings, taking initiative, participating in **on-call rotations**
