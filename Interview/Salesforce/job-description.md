## What Is a Salesforce Org?

A Salesforce "org" (short for organization) is a unique, isolated instance of the Salesforce platform — essentially a fully configured, multi-tenant environment that a company or developer uses to build, customize, and run Salesforce applications.

Every Salesforce customer, partner, and developer works within their own org. Think of it as a dedicated cloud environment scoped to a single entity, complete with its own data, configuration, users, metadata, and running application logic.

The moment someone signs up for Salesforce — whether they're a Fortune 500 enterprise, an independent software vendor, or a solo developer — an org is provisioned for them. **That provisioning is what the Signup team builds and operates.**
