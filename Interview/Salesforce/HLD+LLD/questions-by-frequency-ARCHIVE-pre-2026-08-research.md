##  Key Patterns from the Data

**Notification Service is the single highest-probability question** — confirmed both as HLD AND as LLD in the same 90-min round (Roundz SMTS report). The round went: 40 min HLD → same interviewer said "now show me the class design and APIs." It is the only problem confirmed in the exact HLD+LLD combined format that matches this round.

**The top-3 LLD problems** (Notification, Parking Lot, Rate Limiter) also appear as HLD topics — Salesforce frequently goes bidirectionally on the same problem in the same round.

**Rental systems** (Bike Rental, ZoomCar) showed up in 2 SMTS-specific threads — underrepresented in most prep lists but clearly in rotation.
